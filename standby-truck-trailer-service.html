Service Overview

AMCOL Haulage & Logistics offers a stand-by truck and trailer service to support on-call and time-sensitive transport needs. The service is coordinated through AMCOL's dispatch team so customers throughout Trinidad and Tobago can request truck and trailer support when it's needed.

What This Service Includes

On-Call Support When Timing Matters

A stand-by truck and trailer service means transport resources can be coordinated for customers with urgent or short-notice cargo needs. AMCOL's dispatch team reviews the request and arranges an available truck and trailer combination suited to the cargo.

  • Time-sensitive deliveries
  • On-call transport requests
  • Short-notice pickups
  • Standby coverage for planned operations
  • Cargo requiring flexible scheduling
